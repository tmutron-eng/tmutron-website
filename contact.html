05 / Getting in

How to
join us

01
Join the Discord
Start here. Announcements, study groups, event sign-ups and general chaos all live in the server.
02
Show up to events
There is no membership process, just come through. Study halls, workshops and socials run all year.
03
Get on the team
Want to help run events, write course guides or lead a workshop? Message us on Discord or email us.
04

Taken a course already? Write a review, or upload old exams and notes through the drop box on the Academics page.
