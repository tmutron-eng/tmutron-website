The Mechatronics Course Union at TMU. Study halls and mock exams for the hard courses, hands-on workshops, and a Discord where upper years actually answer.

Explore events Meet the team

300+ active members. 10+ events a year. One union, founded in 2025.

What we run

Everything here is run by Mechatronics students, for Mechatronics students.

  • Academic supportStudy halls and mock exams before midterms, plus course guides written by students who just took the course.
  • WorkshopsPCB soldering, SolidWorks, ROS2 and Raspberry Pi. Short hands-on sessions run by students and other TMU clubs.
  • Course resourcesPast exams, lecture slides and mock finals in one shared drive, organized by course.
  • Social eventsMovie nights, and a mechanical bull night that raised money for CAMH.
  • Game nightsMarvel Rivals tournaments and more. Mechatronics students are competitive about everything, not just circuits.
  • CommunityOur Discord is where announcements, study groups and event sign-ups happen.
