What we run

Academic support

Study halls and mock exams before midterms, plus course guides written by students who just took the course.

Workshops

PCB soldering, SolidWorks, ROS2 and Raspberry Pi. Short hands-on sessions run by students and other TMU clubs.

Course resources

Past exams, lecture slides and mock finals in one shared drive, organized by course.

Social events

Movie nights, and a mechanical bull night that raised money for CAMH.

Game nights

Marvel Rivals tournaments and more. Mechatronics students are competitive about everything.

Community

Our Discord is where announcements, study groups and event sign-ups happen.
